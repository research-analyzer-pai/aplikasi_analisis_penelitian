# Research Analyzer Professional v4.1

## Advanced Research Diagnostics
- Quantitative diagnostics: Cronbach's alpha with corrected item-total correlation and alpha-if-deleted.
- Factorability screening: KMO and Bartlett's test of sphericity.
- Qualitative evidence audit: segment coding coverage, code frequency, orphan coding IDs, and uncoded segment review.
- Streamlit interface tab for the new diagnostics.

## Methodological notes
- KMO/Bartlett are screening diagnostics and do not decide factor retention or establish construct validity.
- Cronbach's alpha is not proof of unidimensionality; inspect item content and factor structure.
- Uncoded segments are review candidates, not automatically negative cases.
- Coding coverage is not theoretical saturation or qualitative quality.
- Existing PLS-SEM remains an exploratory prototype, not a validated replacement for SmartPLS, AMOS, or lavaan.
