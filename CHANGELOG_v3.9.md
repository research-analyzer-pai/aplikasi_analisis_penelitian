# v3.9 – Manuscript & APA Intelligence
- Heuristic extraction of author-year citations and reference entries.
- Audit lists for unmatched in-text citations, uncited reference entries, possible duplicate references, and DOI formatting review.
- APA reference completeness checklist.
- Manuscript readiness checklist with user-configurable sections, abstract limit, and keyword range.
- Journal-specific manuscript outline from requirements manually supplied by the researcher.

Limitations: this is a heuristic text checker, not a complete APA 7 validator. It does not query Crossref, resolve DOI, verify article existence/indexing, or establish journal acceptance likelihood. Complex multi-author citations, Indonesian names, organizational authors, translated titles, and unusual reference formats need manual review.
