## Scope and limitations
The coding recommender is a TF-IDF lexical similarity baseline, not a generative or validated semantic AI model. Similarity scores are not probabilities or evidence of code correctness. Axial/selective coding, theme development, negative-case interpretation, triangulation, and integration remain researcher decisions. Joint display joins only on labels explicitly harmonized by the researcher. PLS-SEM remains the v3.4 prototype and has not been upgraded to SmartPLS-equivalent estimation in this release.
